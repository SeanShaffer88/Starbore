# Starbore

An offline, ad-free idle "planet driller" for Android. It plays like Auto Drill: Galaxy but has its own look: neon holo-hex planets on a violet starfield.

- No INTERNET permission, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, no third-party libraries.
- About 105 KB APK, Android 5.0+ (API 21), version 1.4.1. Portrait or landscape, phones and tablets.

## Gameplay

| System | How it works |
|---|---|
| Auto-mining ship | Flies itself: picks the nearest hex, circles it and fires every unlocked weapon. Drag anywhere to steer with a joystick. Use AUTO/MANUAL to turn autopilot on or off. Your ship can only fly through space you have already mined. |
| Planets | A hex grid with an uneven edge in three layers (crust, mantle, core). **Gold** hexes pay 12x, **cyan bolt** hexes give energy, **red** hexes explode and chain into their neighbours. MAP zooms out to show the whole planet. |
| Planet modifiers | About 45% of planets from sector 5 on have one: **Low Gravity** (shots fly 50% farther), **Magnetic** (bolts home in), **Frozen** (frost armour on every hex, +40% coins), **Unstable** (4x red hexes), **Motherlode** (2.5x gold). |
| Ore veins | Winding seams of rainbow hexes that pay 25x. Finishing a whole vein gives energy and scrap. They glow in MAP view. |
| Boss planets | Every 10th sector, cycling through four types: **Core Titan** (armoured shell and a giant heart), **Aegis World** (green shield nodes; the planet takes only 10% damage until they fall), **Gunship Planet** (turrets fire red stun orbs you can dodge), **Splitter** (break the heart and it splits into two planets, +45 s). Boss planets regrow blocks and have a 150 s limit. If you fail, you farm the previous sector until you tap **FIGHT BOSS**. Bosses drop guaranteed Epic+ gear and hulls. |
| Ship hulls | Wasp, Hornet (+25% speed), Carrier (+1 drone slot), Reaper (bigger, stronger saws), Prospector (+20% coins), Bastion (+15% damage, stun-immune). The first boss always drops one, later bosses have a 50% chance. |
| Comets | Every 45–95 s a comet or a meteor shower flies past. Tap one to catch it: **Frenzy** (fire rate x2), **Gold Rush** (coins x3), **Overdrive** (damage x2.5), an energy cache, or a coin burst from meteors. Not required, but it rewards watching. |
| Research (LAB tab) | 8 projects with prerequisites, costing energy and taking real minutes to hours. They keep running while the app is closed: +damage, +coins, energy cells, drone AI, more boss time, better offline earnings, overclock, and stronger gear. |
| DPS readout | Live DPS in the top bar, a peak-DPS figure (all weapons firing), and a green "+X% DPS" on every weapon, ship, drone and gear upgrade so you can see which purchase is best. |
| Run upgrades | Every cleared planet (bosses: two) earns a pick: choose 1 of 3 cards such as Hot Barrels (+12% damage), Arc Relay (+1 chain jump), Cluster Shells (+1 mortar shell) or Midas Touch (gold +50%). They stack for the current run and reset on Core Collapse. Auto-buy picks for you. |
| Supernova | An active ability (bottom-left button) that vaporises everything near the ship. 40 s cooldown, shortened by the Star Heart card. |
| Deep Core | Unlocks at sector 50. An endless descent where each layer is 25% tougher, with energy and scrap per depth and a depth record. |
| Weekly challenge | One rule each week: Deep Freeze, Powder Keg, Blaster Only, Glass Cannon (90 s), Titan World or Drone Wars. Clear it in time for 50 energy and an exclusive cosmetic. |
| Cosmetics | 6 engine trails (including Rainbow), 5 ship paints and 3 explosion styles (Classic, Plasma, Confetti), unlocked by achievements and weekly challenges. |
| Codex | Every block type, boss, modifier and synergy, with a line of lore. Each entry is revealed when you first encounter it. |
| Auto-buy | The Quartermaster perk (3 cores) unlocks an AUTO-BUY toggle that keeps buying the cheapest upgrade. |
| 10 weapons | Pulse Blaster, Drill Lance, Scatter Cannon, Arc Mortar, Chain Arc, Orbit Saws, Seeker Swarm, Rail Driver, Nova Pulse, Singularity. Every unlock permanently multiplies **all** damage by 1.5. Damage doubles at every 25th level. |
| 6 weapon synergies | **Overload** (Chain Arc makes red hexes blow up x2 and wider), **Napalm** (Mortar + Scatter: burning ground), **Shatter** (Lance ignores armour and breaks frost), **Detonator** (Nova sets off red hexes instantly), **Gravity Grind** (Singularity + Saws: x3 saws), **Conductor** (Rail + Chain: every rail shot releases a free arc). |
| Escort drones | Miner, Gold Hound, Spark Hound, Sapper. Each one hunts its own kind of hex across the planet. You start with 1 slot and get more from the Drone Bay perk. |
| Skills | Roll with energy, then fuse 3 duplicates into the next rarity (Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary). You get 3–6 slots. |
| Gear | Hull (coins), Reactor Core (damage), Engine (fire rate and speed). Salvage spare items for scrap, spend scrap to level up what you have equipped, or forge new items with energy. |
| Core Collapse (prestige, LAB tab) | From sector 25: reset sectors, coins, weapons, ship and drones in exchange for **Cores**. Each lifetime core gives +2% damage. Perks: Core Resonance, Core Yield, Head Start, Drone Bay, Deep Scan (offline), Weapon Memory, Quartermaster (auto-buy). |
| Daily challenge | A planet chosen by the date, with its own modifier, timed against your own best. The first clear each day gives energy and scrap. |
| Achievements and stats | 19 achievements that reward energy and unlock themes and cosmetics, plus a full stats page. |
| Themes | **Neon** (default), **Blueprint** (sector 15), **Pixel** (sector 30), **Paper-cut** (first collapse). |
| Game feel | Brief hit-pauses and slow-motion on big moments, smooth camera shake, blocks that flinch and crack, ember-hot mined blocks that cool into scorch marks, explosion flashes with smoke and sparks, a combo meter (up to +50% coins, with rising pitch), coins and energy that fly into the HUD counters, muzzle flash and recoil, an engine trail, a banking ship, warp streaks between planets, a "CLEARED" stamp, sliding panels and rolling counters. Neon and Blueprint glow additively; Pixel adds scanlines. |
| Onboarding | Short pop-up hints point at each tab as it becomes useful (first upgrade, skills, gear, lab, comets, collapse, bosses). |
| Accessibility | Colour-blind palette (Okabe-Ito colours plus distinct symbols for gold, energy, red, vein, shield and turret blocks) and a large-text option. |
| Performance mode | Fewer particles, no glow, vignette or ghost hexes, and a simpler planet. The game suggests it if frames keep dropping. |
| Sound, music and vibration | Every sound is generated by the game itself, so there are no audio files. Music is a synthwave loop (pads, bass, arpeggio, hats) synthesised in real time; a kick-drum boss layer fades in on boss planets. Sound, music and vibration each have a toggle, and sound effects have a 5-step volume setting (default 60%, below the music). |
| Title screen | An animated logo with your progress summary. Tap to start. |
| Save backup | MORE > SETTINGS > EXPORT / IMPORT SAVE writes or reads a `.json` file through Android's file picker, with no permissions. Imports are validated before they replace your save. |
| Robustness | If drawing fails three times in a row, the error is logged and the game switches itself to performance mode instead of showing a frozen screen. |
| Idle | Saves every 15 s and when the app is backgrounded. While you're away you earn 50% of your mining rate for up to 8 h (more with Deep Scan and Long-Range Sensors). |
| Quality of life | EQUIP BEST for skills and gear, hold any upgrade button to keep buying, and compact panels. |

Privacy: no INTERNET permission, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, no third-party libraries. The only permission is VIBRATE, which never shows a prompt.

## Building

**Android Studio / Gradle:** open the folder and run `app`. No dependencies are needed beyond the Android Gradle Plugin.

**Without the SDK** (how `build/Starbore.apk` was made):

```bash
sudo apt install aapt apksigner zipalign dalvik-exchange   # plus a JDK 17+
./build.sh            # → build/Starbore.apk (signed with a local self-made key)
```

To install, copy the APK to your phone and allow "install unknown apps", or run `adb install build/Starbore.apk`.

## Code map

```
app/src/main/java/com/starbore/game/
  MainActivity.java  lifecycle, offline earnings, immersive mode
  Audio.java         generated sound effects (SoundPool) and vibration
  GameView.java      SurfaceView game loop thread and touch queue
  Game.java          the whole simulation: ship AI, weapons, synergies, drones, bosses, daily, prestige, economy
  Planet.java        hex-grid planet generation (axial coordinates): modifiers, veins, boss shells
  Render.java        world rendering in 4 themes (particles, beams, drones)
  Music.java         real-time synthesised soundtrack (AudioTrack)
  Ui.java            HUD, tabs, scrolling panels, joystick, popups (drawn on the canvas, immediate mode)
  Defs.java          tuning tables: weapons, skills, gear, ship, perks, drones, synergies, achievements, themes,
                     hulls, research, boosts, run cards, mutators, cosmetics, codex lore
  Save.java          JSON save in SharedPreferences
```

All balance numbers are in `Defs.java` and `Planet.java` (block HP curve).

## Testing

There's no device in the build environment, so the game logic was tested on a desktop JVM:

- **Headless bot, 4 simulated hours:** reached sector 290, with one Core Collapse and the post-collapse run back to 290 within about an hour. All four boss types appeared about 13 times each (including the split), 52 bosses were beaten, all hulls dropped, it caught 239 comets and completed 57 research levels, and it made daily runs, run-card picks and Supernova casts. No exceptions. A simulation step averages about 0.04 ms.
- **Feature checks (31):** run cards, Supernova and its cooldown, Deep Core (deeper and tougher layers, surfacing back to the campaign), weekly challenges (all six mutators, reward unlock, time-out failure), cosmetic unlocks, codex discovery, the post-100 damage curve, and a **save round trip** with real `org.json` (including new fields, cosmetics validated after stats load, identical planet layout, and garbage input falling back safely).
- **Earlier checks:** stuns (Bastion immune; about 1 stun a minute when idling on a Gunship), shield nodes, collapse, research timing, auto-buy, comets and Equip Best.

Still needs a real phone: rendering, touch, frame rate, music and sound volume, landscape layout, and the export/import file picker.
